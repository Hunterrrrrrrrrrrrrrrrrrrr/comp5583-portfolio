LoRA inference on Spot instances

Source: Yuhang's 2026 undergraduate thesis, 基于 Spot 实例的 LoRA 推理部署优化研究, abstract (PDF page 2), high-reuse comparison (page 10), section 5.5 (page 15), and conclusion (page 16).

The abstract names the four scheduling strategies. Section 5.5 describes a single-machine, multi-process TCP worker prototype with Thread.sleep task durations and kill -9 interruption. The conclusion limits it to mechanism-level validation, rather than real cloud scheduling or billing and GPU performance predictions.

Cache-aware scheduling benefits depend on reuse and queueing conditions. Simulated results and prototype observations should not be presented as production savings.
